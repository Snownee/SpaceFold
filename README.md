# Space Fold

Space Fold lets you compact a full-scale production line into your personal space shard.

Since a folded machine no longer ticks its thousands of real blocks, only its
measured rates are simulated, huge builds stop tanking your framerate and tick
time.

## How to Play:

First, you’ll need a **Spacetime Anchor**, which will locate and assign you a 9×9×9 spatial shard in an unknown dimension where you can freely build machines. Use the **Spacetime Awl** on the walls to designate resource input and output locations.

Then, click "Trial" in the Spacetime Anchor UI to measure the space's resource throughput (during the trial, resources are fed into the Spacetime Anchor from the outside, with each face corresponding to an out-of-bounds port within the space).

Once the trial is complete, you can use the **Shard Carver** to consume the materials required for construction and mass-produce **Shard Engines**. These engines will continuously accept and produce resources at the rate observed during the trial, without requiring a large amount of space.
